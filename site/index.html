Why this exists

A Cardputer is a lovely little computer, but its screen is 1.14 inches and its keyboard is a grid of pebbles. Most people already carry a far better screen, keyboard and GPS in their pocket. droidputer lets an ESP32-S3 borrow them over a USB cable: the ESP runs the app, the phone draws its pixels, types its keys and feeds it position, and the phone never computes anything for it.

The catalog covers the Cardputer ecosystem: open-source apps rebuilt on demand against a small shim, and prebuilt firmware from the LauncherHub feed. This page exists to answer one honest question: is anybody using it, and how?

What is collected. Only from phones whose owner tapped "Send" on first launch: anonymous events (app opened, catalog opened, build requested, firmware flashed, ESP linked, minutes mirrored, verdict sent, crashes), each tagged with a random per-install id, the app version and the phone model. Never location, accounts, keystrokes or screen contents. The build proxy also counts build requests without any id. Data is stored on PostHog Cloud. Full privacy policy.
